# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Engineering recruiters, internship reviewers, technical collaborators, and mentors evaluating Daniel Shayo's skills, judgment, and growth potential.

## Product Purpose

Present Daniel's engineering background and active projects clearly enough that a reviewer can understand what he builds, what is working, what remains in development, and how to contact him.

## Positioning

The portfolio shows practical systems built around Tanzanian education, community finance, and football analysis, with implementation evidence and limitations instead of inflated completion claims.

## Operating Context

Most visitors will skim on desktop or mobile, open one or two projects, inspect GitHub, and download the CV while comparing internship candidates.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript hosted with GitHub Pages.
- Preserve existing project descriptions, public links, contact details, education, and Cisco training facts.
- Projects are prototypes under active development.
- Do not invent users, commercial adoption, clients, production deployment, or performance claims.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Daniel Deogratius Shayo. The voice is direct, technically curious, honest about unfinished work, and grounded in useful systems.

## Product Principles

- Show the work before describing ambition.
- Separate implemented evidence from future direction.
- Make professional links unmistakable.
- Keep every claim supportable.
- Work equally well for fast scanning and deeper inspection.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, responsive reflow, descriptive links, and meaningful image alternative text.
